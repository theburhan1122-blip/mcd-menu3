Top 8 McDonald's Items You Must Try in 2026

Big Mac®

The king of McDonald's Menu. Three buns, two patties, special sauce, lettuce, cheese, pickles, onions — it's messy perfection. McDonald's Big Mac prices have crept up over the years, but one bite and you remember why it's legendary.

Spicy McCrispy™

Newer kid on the block, but already a fan favorite. Juicy chicken fillet, spicy seasoning, crinkle-cut pickles on a buttery potato roll. If you like heat without crying, this is your sandwich.

Chicken McNuggets® (10 pc)

Golden, crispy, dippable joy. Sweet 'N Sour or Spicy Buffalo? The debate never ends. Pro tip: order 20 pc and pretend you're sharing.

Quarter Pounder® with Cheese

Real beef that actually tastes like beef. Fresh (never frozen) patty, melted cheese, onions, pickles — simple but hits every time.

World Famous Fries

Hot, salty, perfectly crispy outside, fluffy inside. Accept no substitutes.

Egg McMuffin®

The sandwich that invented fast-food breakfast. Fluffy egg, Canadian bacon, melted cheese, English muffin. Still under $5 in most places — insane value.

McFlurry® with OREO®

Soft serve + crushed Oreos = childhood in a cup. The machine is working more often now (fingers crossed).

Iced Coffee

Sweet, creamy, caffeinated happiness for under $3. My personal daily driver.

10 Most Asked McDonald's Menu FAQs

1. What's the cheapest meal on the McD menu right now? +

The $5 McValue Meal Deals (McChicken or McDouble combos).

2. How much is a Big Mac in 2026? +

Usually $6.49–$8.39 depending on location; meal around $9–$11.

3. Does McDonald's still have the 2 for $6 deal? +

It comes and goes — check the app; currently replaced by Buy One, Add One for $1 in many places.

4. When does breakfast end? +

Most locations stop at 10:30–11:00 am weekdays, 11:30 am weekends.

5. Can I get an Egg McMuffin after breakfast? +

Officially no, but some 24-hour locations serve limited breakfast all day.

6. Are McDonald's fries vegan? +

In the US, no — they contain natural beef flavoring (milk derivative).

7. How many calories in a 10-piece McNuggets meal? +

Around 1000 calories with medium fries and drink.

8. Is the McRib coming back in 2026? +

Usually late fall — follow the app for announcements!

9. Do prices vary by state? +

Yes — California, New York, and big cities are usually $0.50–$1.50 higher.

10. What's the best deal in the McDonald's app right now? +

Free medium fries with any $1 purchase every Friday + daily rotating offers.
